# Lockliel flagship design proposals

Local owner review only. These are five connected **proposed** flows P1–P5, plus an explicit recipient-accessibility view. They are not the five canonical journeys or a reconciliation of the unavailable 120-target register.

## Open the interactive preview

Double-click `Open-Flagship-Preview.command`, or run:

```sh
/opt/homebrew/opt/node@24/bin/node /Users/davesmacbookpro/.codex/worktrees/lockliel-flagship-design-proposals/Lockliel/review/flagship-proposals/preview.mjs
```

Open the printed `http://127.0.0.1:PORT` address. Press Control-C to close it. Nothing installs or opens externally. The launcher validates exact asset/source bytes in `integrity.json`, serves only listed static files, accepts only local GET requests and denies external connections and form submissions through CSP. Integrity establishes byte consistency, not owner design approval.

## Review routes and meaningful interactions

| Proposal | Hash route | Local interactions |
|---|---|---|
| P1 My Space | `#space` | Rapid resource search, linked next steps, keyboard reorder/reset, persistent tools |
| P2 Share Hope | `#share` | Search/filter, resource preview, relationship-specific consent, fixed permitted channel, optional message, explicit confirmation, recipient preview |
| P3 Faith Boost | `#boost` | Seven series, visibly selected scheduled/live/replay layouts, inactive player/archive/reminder/notes concepts |
| P4 My Five | `#five` | Five invented relationships, separate attribution/assignment/access facts, private reminder ideas and note feedback |
| P5 My Journey | `#journey` | Protected-course concept, overview/HTML worksheet/private-note variants, bounded local feedback |
| Cross-cutting recipient | `#recipient` | No-account resource introduction, larger text/text-only, privacy and noncoercive next steps |

Use “All my tools” for explicit supporting concepts: Prayer, Scripture, Groups, Founders 50, Resources, Giving, Leadership, Privacy and Administration. These are design-boundary panels, not replacements for their accepted implemented components. The original full member preview and every feature branch remain unchanged. This standalone proposal does not claim functional parity with the complete application.

All input is for invented words only. No browser storage, real member data, credentials, account operation, delivery, payment, schedule, enrollment, course completion, persistent note, upload or service activation is implemented. Refreshing discards state. A relationship selection is not contact consent. Unknown or denied sample permission blocks share review. Final confirmation says “Nothing was sent” and records no share event.

## Design authority

The Mountain Journey composite is an owner-approved inspirational reference, not an approved screen. The new hierarchy emphasizes Share Hope, My Space, My Journey, My Five and daily Faith Boost at 7 PM Central. Plain “Lockliel” text is a temporary label, not a redraw or certification of the official wordmark. Neither the conflicting repository logo nor the unverified candidate master is substituted.

The authoritative palette supplied in prior owner instructions is #007BFF, #0056C9, #E6F2FF, #1A1A1A and #F4F6F8. Supporting navy and muted tones are proposed accessible implementation choices. Exact brand-guide role/spacing/artwork comparison remains pending the local reference ZIP. Poppins Regular and SemiBold are actual static bundled fonts; Permanent Marker is not used without verified role guidance.

Three unchanged real photographs and licensed font files come from independent asset package `c620c9aa2e50ba851459b88af9ecda45cd8bab87`. See `asset-copy-map.json` and `assets/rights-manifest.json` for sources and licenses. A file being licensed does not constitute owner approval of its placement or final production approval. No AI photographic scenes, composite-extracted photos or Pray.com artwork are used.
