Status policy:

- Live and Verified: name the exact externally observed behavior and date. Source deployment alone is insufficient.
- Implemented but Not Released: candidate source/tests exist, but there is no evidence of approved production activation.
- Partially Implemented: foundation exists with material behavior/acceptance gaps. May include published source whose connected behavior is unverified.
- Approved but Not Started: authoritative product direction exists, but no implementation was located; implementation authorization remains separate.
- Proposed / Decision Pending: unresolved product/provider/legal/visual choice; historical conversation alone is insufficient.
- Blocked: a concrete dependency prevents that feature's next required operation. Independent lanes continue.

checks separately, real-vs-synthetic evidence, deployment context, schema compatibility,
security/privacy limits, rollback and approval. Never include credentials, tokens,
member identifiers or raw sensitive prayer/financial/assessment content in evidence.
