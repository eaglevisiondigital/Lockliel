# LOCKLIEL — Master Experience & Mockup Register (Design Baseline)

**Version:** 2026-11-03 — strategy reconciliation candidate. **Status:** OWNER VISION RECORDED; no visual screen below is owner-approved merely by appearing here. **Purpose:** trace every discussed capability into a visible experience before redesigning production screens.

## Immutable identity, mission, and experience rules

- **Official mark:** use original owner-provided approved master only. Open-right icon/circle means *there is always room for one more*. Never close/add a right node, flip, rotate, recolor, redraw, or distort. Logo lockup variants only as the supplied guidelines permit.
- **Brand:** Lockliel Blue `#007BFF`, Deep Blue `#0056C9`, Sky Blue `#E6F2FF`, Charcoal `#1A1A1A`, Light Gray `#F4F6F8`; Poppins primary typography, Permanent Marker handwritten accent. Exactly: `REACH. TEACH. TRAIN. DISCIPLE.` and supporting `powered by relationship™`. Multiplication is the outcome, not a silent alteration of the fixed primary tagline.
- **Mission:** empower ordinary believers beyond church walls to reach others, teach truth, train for growth, disciple, and multiply; welcome seekers and all denominations; not itself a church, not MLM, not a conversion quota system.
- **Photography:** published photographic imagery must be authentic, licensed real photography with rights/provenance, never an AI-generated substitute. Nature: Kansas Flint Hills, sunrise/sunset, sea, mountains, trees, forests, sports, families, community, joyful real people. Concepts may use placeholders only, clearly labeled.
- **Visual:** high-tech, state-of-the-art yet warm, alive, peaceful, sparse, easy across ages; brand-stable framework with member-customizable interest, ambiance, accessibility and dashboard priorities. Real sharing cannot be buried.
- **Faith Boost:** current owner directive **daily at 7:00 PM Central**; calendar display should use `America/Chicago` and CST/CDT seasonally. Older notes say Monday–Friday; treat as superseded for current mockups but flag operating-schedule verification. Do not falsely label prerecorded scheduled content as genuinely live.
- **UX:** mobile-first quick-share; one clear next step; recipient content accessible without mandatory account; progressive disclosure. Original inviter/attribution distinct from mentor, group host, church relationship. Privacy/consent before messaging or exposing third-party data. No non-consensual import/invitation.
- **Safety:** existing production/main/course PR and new isolated-auth deployment restrictions remain in force. No mockup is deployment permission. No mock share actually sends.

## Primary end-to-end user stories to demonstrate

**J01 Encounter → Share:** Believer encounters a need; Search or Share Hope → filter needs/age suitability/life stage/interests/language/media → preview video/graphic/talking point/Scripture → share via SMS/email/native share/Lockliel/link/QR with explicit confirmation → recipient opens public resource → explore Jesus/salvation/Kingdom/King Kind/next step → optional consent-based relationship / My Five → group and course → recipient equipped to reach another.

**J02 Personal grow → multiply:** Sign in → choose My Space visual and interests → personalized home/For You → Faith Journey, Bible, Prayer Board, Scripture Anchors, growth/action plans → Getting a Grip or other training → My Five/Share → group → mentor/host support → leader multiplication.

**J03 Faith Boost daily:** See today's episode in Home and global broadcast center → clear 7 PM Central next broadcast, calendar/subscribe/reminder → watch scheduled or live broadcast → persistent player/audio and Scripture/notes → share replay or highlights → discuss with group/founder circle → prayer/Q&A/next step → archives and seven identity series.

**J04 Founder/community:** Interest/application → review/approval → orientation → share with five weekly → gather 2–10+ in homes/coffee shops/workplaces → CONNECT→WATCH→TALK→ACT→MULTIPLY → groups/host training/attendance/leader support → a new group/host forms. This supports, never replaces, local churches.

**J05 Recipient and volunteer accessibility:** No account required to consume public share; accessible experience, optional sign-in, adjustable text/reduced motion, sensitive content/age safeguards, no pressured conversion tracking, consent-dependent contact and follow-up.

# Lockliel — Owner Decisions Superseding Older Register Wording

This is an addendum, NOT a rewrite of `Lockliel_Master_Experience_and_Mockup_Register.md`. Preserve all 120 stable IDs A01–K10 and the five original journeys. Do not infer approval or implementation from an ID's existence.

## Superseding terminology

- Use **My Lockliel** for the member portal.
- Use **Personalize Your Faith Journey** for the main personalization invitation and **Personalize Your Experience** for the feature.
- Use **Make Lockliel Yours** for the action/button.
- Use **My Journey**, **Share Hope**, and **My Five** for the respective experiences.
- Do NOT use the older phrase `My Space` in new user-facing copy; it appears historically in C05 of the baseline register. Do not casually rename internal database identifiers or APIs.

## Owner-required product behavior

- **Daily Faith Boost Broadcast at 7:00 PM America/Chicago**, with correct CST/CDT display, scheduled/live/replay integrity, archive, Scripture, sharing, discussions and reminders. Verify operational delivery separately; design requirement is not proof of a live integration.
- Relationship-powered Gospel sharing is central. Prioritize rapid need-based discovery, resource preview, intentional SMS/email/native/Lockliel/link/QR sharing, recipient pages without forced signup, My Five follow-up, small-group connections and multiplication. No MLM recruitment or compensation structure.
- Members can personalize look/feel, authentic scenic interests, sports/hobbies, dashboard ordering, display accessibility, formats and shortcuts. Preferences are private by default and must not limit access to core capabilities. Cross-device persistence remains a future integration requirement, not a proven live feature.
- Brand master: owner-supplied open-right Lockliel logo geometry and exact official colors/fonts are immutable. Exact PNG candidate hash beginning `a084d9ed` is not yet independently confirmed as the final master file. No approximation, recoloring, rotation or distortion.
- The Mountain Journey Dashboard is a liked concept, **not** a final owner-approved layout. Photography must be genuine, appropriately licensed and rights-cleared, not AI-generated photographic imitation in released work.
