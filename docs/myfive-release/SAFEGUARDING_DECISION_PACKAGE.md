## Public resource boundary

Public Gospel-resource reading is separate from becoming a member or storing/contacting someone. A public visitor may read an accessible resource without granting communication permission. Public access, a self-selected learning path or a family relationship does not authorize storing a minor's information, creating a contact record, importing details or sending a message. No minor workflow is activated here.

## Requested decision sequence

1. Designate accountable privacy/safeguarding owner and reviewer.
2. Confirm initial adult-member AND adult-contact scope and exact acceptable evidence/age threshold.
3. Approve permitted fields, sensitive-note handling and purpose/channel consent evidence.
4. Set actual retention periods and withdrawal/deletion/suppression rules per category.
5. Review implemented enforcement before any new live collection or communication acceptance.

No policy decision is inferred from the SQL cap-removal authorization. More than five relationships is a product rule, not unlimited storage, contact authority or a waiver of safeguarding requirements.
