# Crisis-support interaction requirements for qualified review

November 1, 2026. Requirements only. No crisis advice, classifier, intervention UI,
helpline list, outreach, monitoring or emergency disclosure is implemented or tested
by the Faith Journey preview. No claim of qualified safety approval is made.

## Gate and review ownership

A separately authorized safety review must name the qualified reviewer, product owner,
privacy/security reviewer and localization owner. The approved scope, reviewed copy,
acceptance matrix, jurisdiction/minors policy and revision date must be recorded before
any member-facing intervention or sensitive classification is implemented or released.
Lane 4 can propose the interaction contract; Lane 8 owns privacy/operations policy,
Lane 1 the identity/security boundary, and Lane 3 accessible shared presentation.

## Required interaction decisions

1. Define whether the feature is a member-invoked support link, a reviewed content
   prompt, or another explicitly approved flow. Do not infer that prayer text authorizes
   automated detection, a diagnosis or third-party disclosure.
2. Describe capabilities honestly. Never imply continuous monitoring, guaranteed
   response, confidentiality beyond actual policy or automatic emergency assistance.
   Faith content must not obstruct or replace access to appropriate human support.
3. Specify reviewed urgency levels and plain, non-coercive wording. Do not invent
   risk labels, clinical conclusions, spiritual scores or promises of recovery.
4. Approve localized support-resource sources and their verification cadence before
   showing destinations. Resolve unknown location safely and explicitly; never infer
   emergency jurisdiction from a profile field or silently send location information.
5. Keep the member in control of intentional contact or sharing, subject to an
   explicitly reviewed legal/safety policy. No automatic inviter, leader, manager,
   family, group or external-service notification is authorized by this document.
6. Before any allowed disclosure, define precise recipients, selected information,
   preview/confirmation, revocation limits, purpose, lawful basis and audit retention.
   A generic sharing preference or staff role is insufficient.
7. Define persistence and logging boundaries. Private reflection text and inferred
   health/safety labels must not enter analytics, URLs, referral metadata or ordinary
   admin reporting. No unreviewed model/provider may receive this content.
8. Include explicit minors, accessibility, cultural/language and jurisdiction review.
   Uncertainty must not become a hidden permission grant or a suppressed support path.

## Required future acceptance

- Qualified review of the full copy and all available/unavailable/unknown states.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, focus, reflow/zoom, contrast and reduced-motion acceptance.
- Tested distinction between a resource being shown, intentional contact initiated,
  delivery acknowledged and human assistance actually available. No invented success.
- Synthetic privacy, accidental-disclosure, unknown-location and unauthorized-recipient
  tests, including direct API paths if an API is later authorized.
- Independent review of retention/deletion/export, operator access and any external
  transmission. No real member crisis data is an acceptance fixture.
- A fail-closed release gate and rollback plan for stale resources, provider failure,
  erroneous classifications and inappropriate disclosure, if those capabilities are
  ever separately approved.

No runtime implementation follows from these requirements. This document is not a
qualified safety determination, operational protocol or authorization to intervene.
